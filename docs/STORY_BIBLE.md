# The two ferry lists

Ira Vale returns to the East Bank receiving station looking for her sister Neri, a ferry operator. Two evacuation manifests exist. Both bear genuine signatures. Only one places Neri on the boat. Ira wants to decide which is true. Neri wants drinking water for passengers who may disappear from the public record if Ira succeeds.

## The world rule

Archive Zero is a pressure and timing network built along a tidal estuary. Coupling its clocks to an anomalous carrier aligned small pieces of neighboring histories. Information crossed first; matter crossed later. Infrastructure can lock a local overlap in place, at the cost of making the other version harder to reach. No machine predicts the future reliably. A recording can precede its cause if the network carries it across an overlap. The same pressure trace on two independently maintained instruments is stronger evidence than a single spectacular video.

| 2023–2025 | The same crossing produces two internally consistent lists. Neri keeps carbon copies. | Different passengers remember the same empty seat. |
| 2026 | Autonomous dispatch spreads. The Survey Circle gains access to isolated observatories by providing maintenance crews. | Handwritten route cards disagree with machine receipts. |
| 2027 | Repeating carriers appear in public infrastructure. Some crews hear requests before they are dispatched. | A night operator labels one reel “leave both channels open.” |
| 2028 | One synchronized test extends an overlap across the estuary. A second tide line appears inside dry buildings. | Salt deposits stop at a doorway. Outside, an unidentified hull appears over the array. |
| 2029 | Fictional border fighting, evacuation, fever and failed logistics split the basin. Some stations censor lists to keep ferries running. | Paper ration tokens and two mutually exclusive final departures. |
| 2030 | The public grid falls silent. Ira starts the intake. Its sound is present on the 2019 tape. | Day One ends with the player's decision to broadcast the lists or answer Neri privately. |

## Geography, factions and motives

East Bank is a municipal plant adapted as a receiving station. Across the water, the floating settlement **Soundings** trades clean water for radio batteries and passage. Upriver the **Silt Archive** holds original instruments below an evacuated observatory. Offshore the **Deep Reach** contains persistent overlaps. The highway inland ends at **Relay Nine**, a restricted flight-test facility. These sites share a river, utility network and supply problem.

**Tidekeepers** operate pumps and protect settlements. Their safety policy removes identities from public radio. **Survey Circle** engineers preserved the trigger logs, but fear a panic if both histories become public. **Index House** publishes names so families can reunite. Its geometric insignia borrows the idea of guild secrecy. **Ferry Hands** shelter displaced passengers regardless of which list recognizes them. Let a member of each group make a generous and a selfish decision.

## Evidence levels inside the fiction

| Report | Authorial truth | Payoff and remaining doubt |
|---|---|---|
| A silver UFO circles the dish. | Some hulls are autonomous survey ferries from another branch. | The hull’s markings predate either ferry company. Who commissioned it remains open. |
| “Scaled people” run the archive. | A nonhuman littoral species, the Keel, uses patterned insulating scales. | Propaganda confused its field suits with a shapeshifter folktale. Show a Keel repairing an instrument. |
| The city was built over a secret order. | Several factions reuse a historic-looking symbol as an access mark. | It marks a surveying method. It proves no allegation about a real organization. |
| A leviathan takes the ferries. | The Sounder is an enormous filter feeder from a connected ecosystem. | Hydrophones hear displacement before a creature is visible. Several disappearances have ordinary causes. |
| A behemoth walks inland. | A herd animal leaves deep parallel channels. | A highway settlement mistook its seasonal route for a moving fault. |
| Dinosaurs survived history. | A prehistoric wetland and present basin briefly overlap. | Footprints, pollen and gait offer stronger clues than a blurry clip. |
| The fever turned people into monsters. | Exhausted survivors repeat gestures during local phase interruptions. | Let a recovered patient describe remembering both versions of a room. Their illness and their dignity persist independently of rumor. |

All dates and events in the story are original alternate history. World-war numbering is a disputed in-world label. Real-world rumors about aliens, fraternities and secret facilities supply genre associations only. No allegation about an actual person or institution is part of the explanation.

## Campaign arc

1. **The Awakening.** Water first. Establish the sisters, the radio and the first reversible interpretation.
2. **Passenger Copy.** Reach the ferry. A public broadcast helps the families find the landing but draws an unwanted crowd. A private reply protects the boat but requires an identification ritual to gain entry.
3. **Low Water.** Recover independent gauges in a flooded district. Choose which settlement gets the one portable pump during the survey.
4. **The Quiet Range.** Explore the flight facility. The apparent invasion resolves into a mixture of mundane prototypes and genuinely unaccounted craft.
5. **Deep Reach.** Chart the Sounder’s passage without disturbing its route. The animal follows the same pressure gradient as the tape.
6. **Both Banks.** Synchronizing the grid would stabilize one shoreline by losing the other. Leaving many small stations unsynchronized preserves crossings but commits communities to shared maintenance. The final decision depends on whose testimony and instruments the player preserved. The sisters can disagree and still recognize one another.

Only Day One is implemented on this branch. Later consequences above define continuity requirements for future saves.
