### Opening, menus, sound and camera

The opening needs a concrete question a player can remember after moving for five minutes. Start with three knocks in a dead pump, then two incompatible ferry lists. End on the shelter radio. The camera cuts between locations in the playable station. Returning control at standing eye height preserves the scale the montage established. Later passes should match a hand movement or sound across a cut, then finish the gesture under player control.

For menus, use the contrast between the station’s large identity lettering and its small equipment labels. Preserve readable button verbs, a distinct focus stroke, a visibly unavailable Continue state and real startup progress. Keep the camera moving slowly behind the menu. Expose the world immediately after Resume. The design borrows the discipline of a low-density game HUD and the functional logic of Metro’s field equipment. No reference interface or artwork was traced.

Sound should give direction and distance before it gives exposition. Begin with transformer hum, keep the pump register distinct, then let three discrete knocks survive across the final cut. A visible transcript preserves the clue with sound muted. The current score and interface tones are synthesized. Spatial propagation, recorded footsteps and performed voices remain production work.

### Film structure

[BFI’s study of Nolan’s chronology](https://www.bfi.org.uk/sight-and-sound/features/christopher-nolan-time-games) gives three distinct tools. *Interstellar* makes elapsed time cost a relationship. Apply that to Ira and Neri losing eleven years. *Inception* makes different temporal rules readable by giving each layer its own location and rhythm. Apply that to the 2019 tape and the live pressure trace. *Tenet* revisits evidence with a changed understanding of direction. The last pump start supplies what the player heard at the beginning. The causal reveal must work if every in-world rumor is ignored. *Children of Men* offers a complementary compositional reading: leave signs of displacement at the edges of a working route, where the player can investigate them voluntarily.

## What the research changed

1. Acquire a licensed, metrically coherent material and prop library before constructing more geography. Keep authored labels, route placement, dish panels and utility networks specific to East Bank.
2. Use real water, power, evidence and transmission state. A menu flythrough cannot demonstrate that the systems work.
3. Preserve two test routes. One player should be able to finish from a new save; another should return after interruption and recognize the station immediately.
4. Benchmark this exact workload. No desktop, mobile or headset target earns a pass from a different engine demo.
