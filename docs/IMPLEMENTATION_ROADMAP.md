# Development gates

| Milestone | Next deliverable | Evidence required to advance |
|---|---|---|
| 0.2 · East Bank | A complete local station loop and a source-controlled asset pipeline | Browser captures and tests of interaction, collision, save and checkpoint return. Record what remains untested. |
| Art lock | One finished shelter, one exterior and one archive work area | Side-by-side captures from fixed positions, close-up surface review, coherent reflection and light directions, clean contact at meter scale, one mobile comparison. Replace repeated masonry, disconnected set dressing and stylized equipment that break the mood. |
| Performance lock | Handheld mesh/texture tiers and district lifetime policy | GPU captures on at least one integrated desktop GPU and one Android phone, 60-second motion samples with p95, texture residency, cold load, repeated restart, context loss and thermal run. Gate expensive reflection/AA effects separately. |
| Full Day One | Playtested 15–30 minute chapter | Three first-time players complete it without verbal direction. Record median duration, where they got lost, how they found the tuning clue, and whether they understood the broadcast decision. Add material consequences, animation and spatial audio before map area. |
| Ferry district | A second district and consequences of both endings | Async preload while walking, bounded peak memory across district transitions, retained evidence on save/reload, route back to shelter. |
| People and creatures | One performed survivor and one physically plausible animal | Review body scale, contact, foot sliding, facial animation and response time. Sound-first distant encounters precede close-up colossal creatures. |
| Headset branch | Comfortable seated and standing interaction | Test actual tracked controllers, physical recentering, locomotion comfort, safety boundaries, stereo overlays, session loss and refresh-rate frame budget. Expand from inspection viewpoints to campaign play only after testing. |
| Connected basin | Multiple crossings, weather and settlement consequences | Evidence continuity across chapters, streaming and save compatibility, day/night legibility, accessible navigation and actual device QA for each new district. |

The historic 0.1 design document remains as a baseline. Current architecture and product scope are defined by this branch’s seven design and QA documents. The Zyberlane XR video player lives outside the game.
